# 🌌 NTE Bond Tracker & Fons Economy Simulator

A highly advanced, standalone progressive web application designed to track and optimize character bond progression in **Neverness to Everness (NTE)**. Avoid wasting *Fons* on bad gifts, track your daily limits, and achieve max bond efficiently.

## 🚀 Features (V4 Premium Overhaul)

*   **18 Official Characters Validated:** Full roster updated to V1.1 (Includes Chaos & Lacrimosa), completely purged of beta placeholder data.
*   **Smart AP Calculator:** Enter your current Level and AP, and the engine automatically backwards-calculates your exact total AP matching the in-game UI.
*   **Fons Economy Engine:** Instantly know which gifts are **[F2P]**, **[Mejor Valor]** (Best Value), or **[Muy Caro]** (Scam).
*   **Dynamic Shop Routing:** The Shop tab renders miniature character avatars right next to the gifts they need, making route planning visual and immediate.
*   **Cloud-like Local Persistence:** Automatically resets your daily gift limits when the date changes without deleting your total AP. Includes full JSON Export/Import functionality for cross-device backups.
*   **Responsive Glassmorphism UI:** Features element-reactive glowing shadows, subtle 3D hover effects, and a custom background noise overlay to mimic the AAA aesthetic of NTE.

## 🛠️ Usage

Simply open `index.html` in any modern web browser. No servers, databases, or installations required. 

1. Select a character using the **Quick Filters** (DPS, Tank, Element).
2. Input your current in-game Level and AP.
3. Check the Fons Efficiency badges and hit "Dar" (Give) to track your daily progression.
4. Check the **Planificador** (Planner) to see your Fons expenditure.
5. Go to **Herramientas** (Tools) to export a backup of your data.

## ⚖️ Economics Disclaimer
The infamous "Bunny Box" is correctly flagged as a highly inefficient gift (20,000 ₣). Use "Algodón Esponjoso" (Fluffy Cotton) or specific 2,000 ₣ items when possible!
